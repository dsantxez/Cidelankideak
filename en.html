Who we are

A candidacy built
on shared values

We are a diverse group of CIDETEC workers who, driven by concerns about certain workplace situations, have decided to step forward and push for the creation of a works council — a process we are currently undergoing.

Getting the process of creating a works council off the ground has not been easy. That is why having achieved it represents an important milestone that everyone should be aware of.

The works council is the body that represents all workers. It is made up of colleagues democratically elected by the workforce, and its main role is to defend collective interests and serve as a link with management. In an organisation like CIDETEC, its role will be key, acting as the voice of the entire workforce on issues that affect our day-to-day working lives.

01

Listening

Listening to the concerns, doubts and proposals of all CIDETEC workers.

02

Transparency

Being informed about and informing on relevant decisions that may affect employment or working conditions (organisational changes, hirings, dismissals, etc.).

03

Impact

Ensuring compliance with labour rights and current legislation (contracts, working hours, safety, equality, holidays, etc.), and improving current working conditions.

04

Occupational Safety and Health

Ensuring compliance with occupational health and safety regulations so that all CIDETEC workers are better protected in their workplaces and against any risk.

05

Heterogeneity

Ensuring equal representation of all CIDETEC workers. This candidacy, led and driven by LAB, is made up of a combination of independent individuals and people affiliated with different unions; likewise, the list has been designed with the aim of equalising representation across job positions.

06

Participation

Taking part in dialogue with management to help improve the working environment, as well as creating various forums that allow us to work more effectively and better defend our collective interests.
